Live streaming turns one moment into reach across platforms—but reliability requires preparation.

## Test connectivity before show day

Run a full rehearsal on the venue network. Confirm upload bandwidth, backup LTE or fiber failover, and encoder settings.

## Use a dedicated stream director

One person should watch program flow, graphics, and platform health while speakers focus on delivery.

## Design for remote and in-room audiences

Hybrid events need clear camera angles, readable slides, and captions where possible. Remote viewers should never feel like an afterthought.

## Prepare lower-thirds and branded overlays

Consistent graphics reinforce professionalism and make replays usable as marketing assets.

## Record a clean ISO feed

Even when streaming live, record a high-quality master file for post-event edits, highlights, and YouTube uploads.

## Promote the replay

Announce where the recording will live within 24 hours to extend the value of your production spend.
