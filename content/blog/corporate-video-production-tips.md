Corporate video projects fail more often in planning than on set. These tips keep shoots efficient and edits smooth.

## 1. Define one primary audience

Write down who must understand the message after watching once. Every script and shot list should serve that person.

## 2. Approve a storyboard early

Align stakeholders on scenes, locations, and interview questions before booking crew. Late changes are the main driver of budget overruns.

## 3. Capture clean audio on location

Viewers forgive imperfect lighting more than bad sound. Use lavaliers or a shotgun mic and monitor levels during interviews.

## 4. Shoot extra b-roll

Wide office shots, product details, and team interactions give editors flexibility and make the final piece feel premium.

## 5. Plan for multiple formats

Capture horizontal master footage, then frame vertical-safe shots for social cut-downs in the same session when possible.

## 6. Build a realistic review window

Include time for legal, brand, and leadership feedback. Rush approvals often create last-minute re-edits.

## 7. Archive project assets

Keep raw footage, logos, and approved masters organized for future campaigns and compliance needs.
