Kenyan audiences scroll fast. A strong opening frame, clear message, and authentic visuals often outperform long copy alone—especially on mobile.

## Stand out in crowded feeds

Short-form video on Instagram, TikTok, and LinkedIn rewards clarity. Brands that show real people, real locations, and real outcomes tend to earn more saves and shares than generic stock footage.

## Explain complex services quickly

Whether you sell software, property, or professional services, video helps prospects understand value before the first call. A 60–90 second explainer can replace pages of text and reduce back-and-forth in sales conversations.

## Build trust with local context

Filming in Nairobi or on location with your team signals credibility. Viewers recognize accents, landmarks, and production quality—and that familiarity supports conversion.

## What to produce first

If you are starting out, prioritize:

1. A homepage hero or brand film
2. Two or three service explainers
3. Client testimonial clips
4. Social cut-downs from longer edits
